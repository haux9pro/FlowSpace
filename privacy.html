Privacy Policy

Flow Space — Your personal productivity companion

Last Updated: May 2026

Introduction

Flow Space ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a Chrome browser extension designed to help you take notes, manage podcasts, organize music from Google Drive, track calendar events, and boost your productivity. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data when you use the extension.

We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data with any third parties. All your data is stored locally on your device and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive account.

Data We Access

🔐 Google Account (via OAuth 2.0)

When you connect your Google account, we request the following permissions:

  • drive.appdata — To back up your SQLite database to a hidden app-specific folder in your Google Drive. This data is invisible to other apps and users.
  • drive.readonly — To read audio files you have manually placed inside a PersonalAssistantExtension/Musics folder in your Google Drive for the music player feature.
  • userinfo.email & userinfo.profile — To display your account info when connected, so you know which account is linked.

We never access your documents, spreadsheets, photos, or any files outside of: the hidden app data folder (for backups), and the PersonalAssistantExtension/Musics folder (for music playback).

🗂️ Note Content (Tiptap Editor)

All notes, notebooks, tags, and rich text content you create are stored:

  • Locally — in an SQLite database synced via chrome.storage.local.
  • In your Google Drive (optional) — only if you enable the backup feature, the entire database is uploaded as a single encrypted blob to your private app data folder.

🎙️ Podcast Subscriptions

When you add a podcast via RSS feed URL, the extension fetches the feed to retrieve episode metadata (title, description, audio URL, duration, artwork). The extension does not host, store, or redistribute any copyrighted podcast content. It simply provides a player interface for the audio files hosted by the original podcast publishers.

Podcast Index API keys (if provided) are stored locally and used solely to search the Podcast Index database at your request.

📅 Calendar Events

Your to-do tasks and personal events (including memorial dates, anniversary reminders, and Vietnamese Lunar calendar dates) are stored locally in your SQLite database. No calendar data is sent to any external server.

Data Storage & Security

  • Local Storage: All primary data is stored in an SQLite database that lives in your browser's local storage. The database is not encrypted at rest by the extension (we rely on your browser's built-in sandboxing).
  • Google Drive Backup: If you enable backup, your database file is uploaded to your private Google Drive app data folder. This folder is only accessible by this extension and cannot be seen or accessed by other apps or users.
  • Authentication Tokens: OAuth tokens are managed by Chrome's chrome.identity API and are never exposed to any third party.

Third-Party Services

The extension communicates with the following external services solely at your request:

  • Google Drive API — For backup/restore and music library access.
  • Podcast RSS Feeds — To fetch episode data from URLs you provide.
  • Podcast Index API (optional) — If you provide an API key, to search for podcasts.

No data is sent to any analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

Data You Control

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Export / Backup: Use the Google Drive backup feature to save your database.
  • Delete: Uninstalling the extension will remove all locally stored data. Google Drive backups can be manually deleted from your Google Drive app data folder.
  • Disconnect: You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from the extension's settings.

Children's Privacy

The extension is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact
