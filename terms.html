Description of Service

Flow Space is a Chrome browser extension that provides the following personal productivity features:

  • Rich Text Notes — A full-featured editor (powered by Tiptap) with support for headings, lists, tables, task lists, inline formatting, tags, and hierarchical notebooks.
  • Podcast Manager — Subscribe to and play podcasts from RSS feeds, search the Podcast Index, and manage your listening queue.
  • Google Drive Music Player — Stream audio files stored in your personal Google Drive.
  • Google Drive Backup — Automatic or manual backup of your data to your private Google Drive app data folder.
  • Calendar & Tasks — Personal events, to-do lists, memorial reminders, and Vietnamese Lunar calendar integration.
  • Dashboard — A centralized bento-grid overview showing statistics, recent notes, and quick actions.
